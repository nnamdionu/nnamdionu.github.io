Business Intelligence Case Study

Olist E-Commerce Analytics

An end-to-end analysis of Brazilian e-commerce performance, combining PostgreSQL business analysis with interactive Power BI dashboards to explore sales, customer satisfaction, delivery efficiency, and seller performance.

PostgreSQL SQL Power BI Power Query DAX Data Modelling

Project Overview

Using the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist, this project examines approximately 100,000 orders and connects transactional, product, customer, seller, delivery, and review information to provide a comprehensive view of marketplace performance.

99,441 Total Orders
R$13.59M Order-Item Sales Value
96,096 Unique Customers
93.23% On-Time Delivery Rate
4.09 / 5 Average Review Score

Business Questions

  • How does order activity and sales value change over time?
  • Which product categories and customer states contribute the most sales value?
  • How reliably are customer orders delivered on time?
  • How does late delivery relate to customer satisfaction?
  • Which sellers and products contribute the most marketplace activity?

Analytical Approach

1. PostgreSQL & SQL

Explored the relational Olist dataset, reviewed data quality, and developed SQL queries to analyze sales, orders, product categories, delivery performance, customer reviews, and seller activity.

2. Data Preparation & Modelling

Imported nine PostgreSQL tables into Power BI, configured relationships, adjusted data types, and standardized product category labels using Power Query.

3. DAX & Dashboard Development

Developed measures for sales value, order counts, unique customers, average order value, delivery punctuality, review scores, active sellers, and product-category performance.

Key Business Insights

  • Sales concentration: São Paulo contributed approximately R$5.2M in order-item sales value, making it the leading customer state.
  • Leading categories: Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts were among the highest-value product categories.
  • Delivery reliability: 93.23% of eligible delivered orders arrived on or before their estimated delivery date.
  • Customer satisfaction: On-time deliveries averaged a review score of 4.29, compared with 2.27 for late deliveries.
  • Seller ecosystem: The dataset includes 3,095 active sellers represented in order-item activity.

Methodology & Limitations

Sales value is calculated from the sum of order-item prices, excluding freight. It represents the value recorded in order items across order statuses and should not be interpreted as accounting-recognized revenue.

Delivery punctuality is evaluated only for delivered orders with both actual and estimated delivery dates. The comparison is made at the calendar-date level. Findings describe patterns in the historical dataset and do not establish causal relationships.
